How to play

Easy to learn, fun to master

  1. 1

    Check the target

    The small picture shows how the board should look. Tap it in the game to see it bigger.

  2. 2

    Slide and twist

    Move a whole row or column one step. Tiles loop around the edges, so plan your twists.

  3. 3

    Fewer moves, more stars

    Solve it in the best number of moves to earn all three stars. Stuck? Ask for a hint.

Questions

Good to know

Is Twist the Tiles free?

Yes. The whole game is free with no in-app purchases. Optional short video ads unlock hints.

Do I need the internet?

No. You can play every level offline. A connection is only needed to watch an ad for a hint.

Is it suitable for kids?

It's rated 4+ on the App Store. There's no chat, no sign-up and no personal data to enter.

I found a bug or have an idea

We'd love to hear it! Email support.twistthetiles@gmail.com or see the support page.
