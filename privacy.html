Privacy Policy

Twist the Tiles · Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Twist the Tiles (“the game”, “we”) uses, and why. The game is made by an independent developer. If you have any questions, contact us at support.twistthetiles@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, or other personal details.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, streak) and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional video ads (to unlock hints) through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, daily streak, and sound, music and vibration settings on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or using “Reset progress” in Settings deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch an ad for a hint, the game uses Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)
  • Your IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crash reports from the ads SDK

Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised:

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for your permission before Google can use your device’s advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device’s Google settings.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you will be asked for consent through Google’s consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Learn more about how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not collect personal data on our own servers, and we do not sell personal data. The only third party that receives data from the game is Google AdMob, for the advertising purposes described above.

Your rights

Because we don’t hold your personal data, most requests can be handled directly on your device (resetting progress, uninstalling, or changing ad and tracking settings). For data processed by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy linked above. You can always contact us with questions or requests.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
